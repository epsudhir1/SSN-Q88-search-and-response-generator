# Prompt Starters

Use these when running the Custom GPT with uploaded files.

1. **Full report generation**
   - “Using the uploaded files, prepare the full Learning From Events report for 1 January 2026 to 31 March 2026 exactly in the required format.”

2. **Data quality first**
   - “Before analysis, show me a data quality audit: missing fields, duplicate records, inconsistent categories, and any outliers needing manual review.”

3. **Incidents only**
   - “Analyze incidents only for 1 January 2026 to 31 March 2026. Show recurring and isolated events, recurring and isolated causes, and top preventive actions.”

4. **Near miss + unsafe only**
   - “Analyze near miss and unsafe act/condition reports only. Group by area and type of work, and map likely consequences strictly to approved incident categories.”

5. **Management brief**
   - “Create a one-page management brief with top 5 recurring themes, top 5 learning points, and top 10 actionables with owners and timelines.”
